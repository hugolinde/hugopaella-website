About Hugo

Who is Hugo?

Hugo, that's me. I have been making paella for over thirty years, but my love for the dish is really about something bigger: Spain, its culture and the joy of eating together.

Spain runs in the family. My parents were already visiting the country in the 1950s, long before a holiday in Spain became the norm. Their stories captured my imagination, and from a young age I went along myself. In this photo you can see me as a four-year-old in Calonge. Even then I felt at home there.

So when I had to choose where to do my internship during my studies, I didn't have to think for long. I went to Tarragona on my own and had a wonderful time there, both at work and outside of it.

Later I lived and worked in Spain again. These days I divide my time between the Netherlands and Tarragona. There, in my own Spanish neighbourhood, I have also made paella for Spaniards, as a Dutchman. And anyone who has ever stood around a paella pan with Spaniards knows how many stories, traditions and opinions come together: about the rice, the stock, the fire and, of course, how it was always done at home. It's exactly those moments at the table and around the pan that have only made my love for paella grow.

Over the years I have studied recipes and books, tried out a lot and, above all, made a great many paellas. A highlight was my visit to restaurant Bon Aire in El Palmar, in the heart of the Albufera near Valencia. There I was allowed to cook in the kitchen with Raúl Magraner, winner of the world paella championship. Yes, that world championship really exists: with professional chefs (even from Michelin-starred restaurants) and qualifying rounds all over the world.

I bring all that experience with me when I cook for a group. But just as important is something I have experienced in Spain since my childhood: paella is a social moment. People gather around the pan, talk about the food, watch the cook and finally sit down at the table together. That is exactly what I want to bring with Hugo's Paella.

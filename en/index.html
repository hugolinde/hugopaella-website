Hugo's Paella

Authentic paella,
freshly cooked on location

Welcome, I'm Hugo, el paellero holandés. I cook authentic paellas completely fresh on location, with good ingredients, traditional techniques and, above all, a lot of attention to flavour.

To me, paella is more than just food. The big pan stands right among the guests and naturally becomes a place where people gather, watch, chat and look forward to what will soon be on the table.

I don't compromise on quality. That's why I deliberately keep the layer of rice in the pan thin, even when I'm cooking for a large group. It gives a better texture and flavour. To safeguard that quality, I cook for groups of up to 50 people.

From Dutchie to Paellero

My bond with Spain started when I was a child and has never faded. To me, a paella symbolises that love. I have been making paellas for more than 30 years. I lived and worked in Spain and kept studying recipes, ingredients and techniques.

A very special highlight was cooking in the kitchen of restaurant Bon Aire in El Palmar, in the heart of the Albufera near Valencia, together with paella world champion Raúl Magraner.

The paella

Paella was born in Valencia, but it has become much more than a famous Spanish rice dish. It is also a way of eating together: one big pan in the middle of the table, to enjoy with family and friends.

On this site I write about the origins, ingredients and techniques behind a good paella. And above all, I encourage you to try it yourself. With a good pan, the right ratio of rice to liquid and a little patience, you can make a beautiful paella at home.

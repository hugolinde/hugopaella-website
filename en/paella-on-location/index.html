Paella on location

Paella is all about sharing

I come to you and prepare the paella fresh on the spot, right in the heart of the gathering. Watch it come together, take in the aromas and have a look as I cook. It’s not just about the food: the cooking itself becomes part of the experience.

I work with fresh and authentic ingredients as much as possible. For the Paella Valenciana, for example, I use rice from Valencia, garrofó beans and D.O. La Mancha saffron. The stock doesn't come from a packet either: it develops during cooking from the fresh ingredients.

And then the rice. A good paella shouldn't have a thick layer of rice. That's why I deliberately go for a thin layer, even in my largest pans. It's also the reason I cook for a maximum of 50 people per group: I'd rather make a better paella than as many portions as possible from one pan.

Frequently asked questions

How does paella on location work?

The paella

What makes your paella special?

First of all, I work with fresh, authentic ingredients and cook the paella completely fresh on location. I even make the stock fresh on the spot.

But what really makes the difference for me is the thin layer of rice in the paella pan. A thicker layer would mean I could serve many more people from one pan, but that comes at the expense of quality and flavour. That's why I deliberately choose a thin layer of rice.

It's also the reason why, despite my large paella pans, I serve groups of up to 50 people. That way I can make the paella the way I believe it should be made.

Is the paella cooked completely fresh on location?

Absolutely. I cook the paella on the spot and completely fresh. I even make the stock on location from fresh ingredients.

I prepare the ingredients earlier in the day, but the actual cooking of the paella happens on location, where you can be part of it.

Which types of paella can I choose from?
  • the traditional Paella Valenciana
  • a paella with fish and seafood
  • the vegetarian Paella de Verduras
  • Fideuà, the version with pasta instead of rice
Do you also have a vegetarian paella?

Certainly. My Paella de Verduras is a fully vegetarian paella, made with fresh vegetables.

Can we choose more than one type of paella?

I prefer to make one big paella for the whole group. The whole idea is that everyone eats together around the same paella pan, just as is traditionally done in Spain.

Would you still like more than one type of paella? Depending on the number of guests per type, I can prepare a second paella pan. Mention it in your quote request and I'll happily see what's possible.

Can the ingredients of the paella be adjusted?

Often, yes. Would you prefer certain ingredients to be included in the paella, or left out? Let me know in advance so I can see what's possible.

There are a few limits, though. I'm a proud paellero, so chorizo doesn't go into my paella. I also don't make paella mixta, in which meat and fish are combined.

Within those limits, I'm happy to help you put together a paella that suits your group as well as possible.

Which ingredients do you use?

I work as much as possible with fresh and authentic ingredients that suit the paella in question.

Want to see exactly what goes into a particular paella? Above you'll find a short description of each dish; the full ingredient lists will follow once the Paella Recipe Calculator is live.

How big are the portions? Is paella a complete meal?

I serve generous portions and allow at least 100 grams of uncooked rice per person. Together with all the other ingredients, that comes to about 600 grams of paella per person.

That makes the paella a complete meal in itself. Starters, side dishes or desserts are certainly not necessary, but of course they're nice. Would you like to extend the meal with tapas, a salad or a dessert, for example? I'm happy to think along with you.

Cooking on location

How does cooking on location work?

For me, the preparation starts a few days in advance, when I order the most important ingredients. That way I make sure, for example, that fresh fish and seafood are available on the day itself and that I have authentic ingredients, such as D.O. La Mancha saffron, in stock in good time.

On the day itself I clean and prepare the ingredients. I transport everything chilled to your location. I arrive about 1.5 hours before the agreed dinner time with the paella pan, gas burners and other equipment. On arrival, I briefly go over the schedule with the host and set up my workspace.

Then the best part begins: I make the paella with you there. Everyone is welcome to come and watch around the pan. When the paella is ready, I place the pan in the centre. Afterwards, I tidy everything up again.

What do you need from us on location?

For each paella pan I need about 2 square metres of workspace, preferably in a spot that is as sheltered from the wind as possible.

I bring my own folding table, cooking equipment, water for the paella and other supplies. I don't need electricity. A water tap nearby so I can wash my hands is handy, though. A parking space close to the cooking spot is of course also welcome.

What happens in bad weather?

When you book, I like to discuss a backup plan for your specific location straight away. Think of a suitable covered area or the option of moving indoors.

What is practically possible in rain, strong wind or low temperatures depends on the location and the conditions. That's why I prefer to agree on this with you in advance, so we don't face any surprises on the day itself.

Can the paella be cooked both indoors and outdoors?

Certainly. I use professional paella burners that are also suitable for cooking indoors. It is important, though, that the indoor space is well ventilated.

Not sure whether your indoor location is suitable? Let me know in advance and I'll happily take a look with you.

Can you also provide music while the paella is being cooked?

Certainly. I can bring a fitting playlist, or share the playlist with you so you can play it yourself.

And if the atmosphere and the moment call for it, I might pick up my guitar for some spontaneous live music while cooking.

Allergies & dietary requirements

Which allergens does the paella contain?

My paellas are made without the 14 allergens defined by EU law. I have checked the ingredients of every paella against a professional allergen matrix, also taking possible cross-contamination into account.

Do you have a severe allergy? Always mention it in advance when booking, so I can go through the specific situation with you.

Is the paella gluten-free?

Yes. My paellas are gluten-free. I work with fresh ingredients and don't use stock cubes, concentrates, spice mixes or processed meat products that may contain gluten or trace warnings in the paella or the stock.

I also take care to prevent cross-contamination. If, besides the paella, I also provide tapas, salads, desserts or bread, for example, I use separate kitchen equipment for those.

Is the paella lactose-free?

Yes. My paellas are lactose-free. A traditional paella never contains milk or dairy products such as butter, cream, cheese or milk anyway.

Because I work with fresh ingredients and don't use ready-made stocks or sauces, I don't add lactose that way either.

Can you take other allergies or dietary requirements into account?

Yes, I'm happy to think along with you. Let me know about allergies and dietary requirements as early as possible when booking. Then I can assess in advance what is possible and what I need to take into account while cooking.

Prices

What does paella catering cost per person?

See the prices page for the price per guest for each dish, and for an indication of the total price for your group.

Is there a minimum order amount?

I don't have a minimum number of guests. A call-out fee always applies, though: € 75 fixed plus € 0.50 per kilometre (return trip). On the prices page you can calculate it instantly based on your postcode.

What is included in the price?

The price includes the paella, my work as a cook, the cooking on location, setting up and clearing away, and transport to and from the location.

Plates, cutlery, glasses and napkins are not included as standard. The same goes for extra dishes such as tapas, salads and desserts, and for drinks such as wine. I can provide these at an extra charge. Let me know in your quote request what you're interested in and I'll include it in the quote.

Do the prices shown include VAT?

Yes. The prices shown include 9% Dutch VAT. I state the VAT separately on the invoice.

How does payment work?

Once the date and the other arrangements have been confirmed, the booking becomes final after the 20% deposit has been received. We'll agree on how and when you pay the remaining amount in the quote.

Booking

For which events can you provide paella on location?

For almost any event where eating together and live cooking fit in. Think of a dinner at home with friends or family, a birthday, garden party, company party, staff party, wedding, festival, or a party at an event venue or on the beach.

Have something else in mind? Let me know. I'm happy to see whether it's practically possible.

How do I book?

Send me an email with your request and include at least the date you have in mind.

Even if your event is coming up soon, feel free to get in touch. If I'm still available, I'll happily see what's possible. Your booking is final once the arrangements have been confirmed and the 20% deposit has been received.

For how many people can I book?

I don't have a minimum number of people. At the top end, I serve groups of up to 50 people.

That maximum is a deliberate choice. I have large paella pans, but I deliberately keep the rice layer thin because that is important for the quality and flavour of the paella.

Can I still change something after booking, such as the number of guests?

A lot is possible by mutual agreement. If you'd like to change something after booking, let me know as soon as possible. I'll then happily look at what can still be adjusted.

Can you also provide additional catering?

Yes, on request I can also provide matching wines or cava alongside the paella. For example, I have good contacts with vineyards in Spanish wine regions. I deliberately don't offer sangria; it doesn't fit the authentic Spanish approach I have in mind.

I can also provide extra dishes, such as tapas, salads or desserts, and bring plates, cutlery and glasses if you wish. Let me know in your request what you're looking for and we'll work out a suitable plan together.

I don't take care of the rest of the event's set-up, such as other drinks and dishes, furniture, buffet decoration and table service. For that, you may need to hire another party.

Terms

What are the cancellation terms?

If you cancel up to four full days before the agreed date, you only lose the deposit. In that case we can also look together at whether the booking can be moved to another date.

If you cancel within four full days of the agreed date, 80% of the total amount will be charged. If you cancel on the day itself, the full amount is due.
